✦ N'ur

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

N'ur helps Bahá'í communities and their friends share gatherings, recaps, and messages. This policy explains what information N'ur collects, how it's used, who can see it, and how you can delete it. We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password (passwords are stored securely and never visible to us or anyone else).
  • Profile: your name, phone number, age group, whether you are an enrolled Bahá'í or a seeker/friend, your Bahá'í ID number if you choose to add it, and a profile photo if you add one.
  • Communities you join, and any city and state you send us if your community isn't listed yet.
  • What you post and do in the app: gatherings (title, time, location, flier, quote, and details), recaps and photos, comments, RSVPs, follows, blocks, reports, and private messages.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show you your communities' gatherings and recaps, and let you RSVP, message, follow, and comment.
  • To show members-only gatherings, like the 19-Day Feast, only to enrolled Bahá'ís.
  • To send you notifications you've asked for, such as new gatherings from people you follow and reminders for gatherings you RSVPed to.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, and act on violations of the Code of Conduct.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we never sell or rent it.

Who can see your information

  • Other N'ur users can see your name, profile photo, member status (enrolled or seeker/friend), age group, communities, follower count, and the gatherings, recaps, and comments you post.
  • Gatherings are shown to members of the community they're posted in. Members-only gatherings are shown only to enrolled Bahá'ís.
  • Private messages are visible only to you and the person you're messaging.
  • Your email, phone number, and Bahá'í ID are not shown to other users.
  • The app's administrators can access information when needed to run the app, investigate reports, or keep the community safe.

Permissions on your phone

  • Photos: only when you choose a flier, recap photo, or profile picture. N'ur doesn't browse your library.
  • Calendar: only to add a gathering you choose to your calendar. N'ur doesn't read your other calendar events.
  • Notifications: to remind you about gatherings you've RSVPed to. You can turn these off in your phone's settings at any time.

Where your information is stored

N'ur's data and photos are stored with Supabase, a secure cloud database provider, which stores them on our behalf. Information is sent between the app and Supabase over encrypted connections.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. Recaps leave the Home feed after 30 days but stay on your profile until you delete them or your account. When you delete your account, your information is removed as described below.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile and login, your profile photo, the gatherings, fliers, recaps, and photos you posted, your RSVPs, comments, follows, community memberships, and your conversations and messages. This can't be undone. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Young people

N'ur is meant for people 15 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. For important changes, we'll let you know in the app.
